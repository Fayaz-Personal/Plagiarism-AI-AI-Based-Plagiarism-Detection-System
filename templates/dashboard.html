Reviewer Guidelines

Interpreting Similarity Scores:

  • < 10% (Low): Standard academic vocabulary and common subject phrasing.
  • 10% – 30% (Moderate): Common terminology, standard definitions, or cited source passages.
  • 30% – 60% (Elevated): Significant overlapping sentence structures; manual instructor inspection required.
  • ≥ 60% (High): Extensive identical passages; evaluate quotation marks and attribution.
